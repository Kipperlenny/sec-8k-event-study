# Research and risk notice

*SEC 8-K Event Dataset + Reproducible Python Event Study, v1. This notice ships in the download and appears on the listing.*

**Historical, not predictive.** Every number in this product describes what happened to listed US stocks after certain Form 8-K filings between 2005 and 2025, under stated assumptions (entry one session after the filing, fixed holding horizons, 20 basis points round-trip cost, a spliced MSCI World proxy as benchmark). Past results, backtests and event studies are not a reliable indicator of future returns. Effects found in this data may have weakened, disappeared or reversed; the benchmark tables themselves show decay in the most recent five years for the restructuring event.

**No advice, no signal, no offer.** Nothing in this product is investment advice, a recommendation to buy or sell any security, a trading signal, or an offer or solicitation to buy or sell anything. The seller is not a registered investment adviser in any jurisdiction. Any decision you take on the basis of this material is yours alone. Shares can lose all of their value.

**Survivorship and coverage.** The event tables come from SEC EDGAR full-text search and are complete for filers that still carry a ticker in EDGAR; filers that were delisted before the data was collected may be missing. The benchmark tables were computed from a price source that has no history for many delisted companies; the number of events without prices is reported per event class, and a stress test that assumes a full loss on every missing event is included. Point-in-time index membership is used for the S&P 500 only; NASDAQ-100 membership is the current list. The event definition is blunt: an 8-K item covers small and large actions alike.

**Costs, taxes and frictions.** Returns are after a flat round-trip cost assumption and before taxes, financing, slippage beyond that assumption, and currency effects. Your results will differ.

**No price data.** The product contains no security prices and no per-trade rows. To recompute or extend the benchmarks you need prices from a source whose terms allow your use.

**No warranty of accuracy.** The tables can contain classification errors, missed filings and ticker-mapping errors. They are provided as is; see the licence for warranties and liability.
