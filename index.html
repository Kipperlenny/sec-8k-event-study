SEC 8-K Event Dataset

Restructuring, impairment and restatement events of S&P 500 companies, 2005 to 2025, with acceptance timestamps and a reproducible Python event study.

What is in it

Every row carries the EDGAR acceptance timestamp, the trading session it fell into (pre_market, market_hours, after_market, non_trading_day), the first full session after it, CIK, accession number, form type, period of report, SIC code, index membership at the filing date and the link to the filing. A separate file lists the 21,654 filings that did not become events, each with its reason.

What is deliberately not in it

No security prices and no per-trade rows. The aggregate statistics were computed from a price source whose terms forbid redistribution, so the package ships statistics rather than prices, and the price series cannot be reconstructed from it. You bring a price source whose terms allow your use; the engine takes any series of adjusted closes.

The code is free

The loader, the event-study engine, the price-provider interface, the tests and the notebook are MIT licensed and public. Read them before you decide whether the data is worth anything to you.

Honest limits

Delisted filers without a ticker in EDGAR are missing from the event list, and delisted companies are missing from the price history; both holes are counted and stress-tested rather than hidden. Index membership is point-in-time for the S&P 500 only. The item-based event definition is blunt. Two data-rights questions, the index-membership column and the derived aggregates, are documented as open rather than asserted away.
